What this plan assumes, and what it carried over unchanged

  • Slot length. Meetings are 45 minutes, working breakfasts 60 and working lunches 75. That cadence comes from the 23 September roadshow plan and is not re-derived here.
  • Drive times. Every leg marked Fact is carried from the 23 September page, which states the mileage and the road behind each. Every leg marked Estimate is new to this plan and is a typical figure, not a measured one.
  • The de-select rule. An institution comes out of the itinerary only when the institution itself is on Carmo's confirmed list. Teacher Retirement System of Texas and Texas Municipal Retirement System qualify. Texas Treasury Safekeeping Trust Company does not, because the Carmo seat is a former employee attending as an unaffiliated individual.
  • What the 23 September page already fixed. The conference is one day, Thursday 22 October, at the Four Seasons Hotel Austin, so Wednesday is a full working day and not a half day around a conference floor. Every address, neighbourhood and intercity leg used here comes from that page.
  • Scoring. Score 5 means a path already held, a live venture programme, named biotech evidence and a seat in the metro. Score 4 drops one of those, usually the biotech commitment. A relationship row means a real relationship in the files, not a database record.
  • What is not here. No flight numbers, no cheque size and no fund figures. The ask lines say what to request, not what to offer.
  • Emails. Contact email addresses are in the workbook's Contacts sheet only.
